DAMU Server Builder - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how DAMU Server Builder ("DAMU", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our Discord server building and management service.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on how you use DAMU, the Service may process limited Discord information required to provide its functionality.

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord username or display information
  • Discord server/guild ID
  • Server configuration information
  • Channel, category, and role information
  • Permissions required to perform requested actions
  • Commands or interactions sent to the Service
  • Technical information required for security and troubleshooting

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by DAMU may be used to:

  • Create and configure Discord servers.
  • Create channels, categories, roles, and permissions requested by users.
  • Respond to commands and interactions.
  • Maintain service security and reliability.
  • Diagnose errors and technical problems.
  • Prevent abuse or unauthorized use of the Service.

4. Data Storage and Retention

DAMU only retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, or meet applicable legal requirements.

Information that is no longer required may be deleted or otherwise removed from our systems.

5. Discord

DAMU interacts with Discord through Discord's services and APIs. Discord may independently process information according to its own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

DAMU does not control how Discord processes information.

6. Third-Party Services

DAMU may use third-party infrastructure or services to operate, maintain, secure, or improve the Service.

Where third-party services process information on behalf of DAMU, their processing may also be governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by DAMU. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of personal information associated with their use of DAMU where applicable.

Requests should include enough information for us to identify the relevant Discord account or server and determine what information should be removed.

9. Children's Privacy

DAMU is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. Users should use the Service only where permitted by applicable laws and Discord's requirements.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Service, its data practices, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact the DAMU Server Builder development team through the project's official communication channels.
