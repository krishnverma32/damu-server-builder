2 Description of Service & Features

DAMU Server Builder is a Discord utility and automation application designed to help community owners and moderators organize, build, and run Discord communities. Features include:

  • Server Builder & Templating: Generation and automated creation of Discord categories, text channels, voice channels, forum channels, and permission overwrites from predefined templates or JSON schemas.
  • Support Ticket System: Creation of private support ticket channels with button-driven interactions, transcript generation, staff notifications, and automated channel cleanup upon closing.
  • Moderation Tools: Kick, ban, timeout, warn, clear, channel lock/unlock, and slowmode commands.
  • AutoMod & Raid Shield: Automated filtering for link spam and burst image flooding with temporary restrictions.
  • Member Verification & Auto-Roles: Button-based verification gates and automatic role assignment for newly joined members and bots.
  • AI Assistant Chat: Interactive conversational assistant powered via OpenRouter with per-user conversational memory and personas.
  • Activity & Leveling: Message-based activity tracking, XP calculation, level progression, and guild statistics.
